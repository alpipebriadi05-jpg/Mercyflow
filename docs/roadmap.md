# Mercyflow Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Professional public website and product identity.

## Phase 2 — Transparency
Campaigns, contribution records, and public impact dashboard.

## Phase 3 — Wallets
Non-custodial wallet connectivity and transaction tracking.

## Phase 4 — Blockchain
On-chain verification and smart-contract infrastructure.

## Phase 5 — Ecosystem
Governance and token mechanics only when the product and community justify them.
